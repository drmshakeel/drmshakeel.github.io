I am a Scientist at [Honda Research Institute Japan Co., Ltd.](https://www.jp.honda-ri.com/en/members/muhammad-shakeel/), where I advance next-generation speech capabilities. My research focuses on multilingual speech processing, specifically [multi-speaker ASR and speaker diarization](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2508.20474). I specialize in [contextual biasing for personalization and advancing speech model architectures](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2601.22792).

Most recently, I collaborated with [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu) on the [Open Whisper-style Speech Model (OWSM)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.00338) project, advised by [Prof. Shinji Watanabe](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=U5xRA6QAAAAJ&hl=en).

I hold a PhD from the [Tokyo Institute of Technology](https://www.isct.ac.jp/en) (now Institute of Science Tokyo), where I was advised by [Prof. Kazuhiro Nakadai](https://researchmap.jp/nakadai). My academic foundation includes a collaboration with the [ALICE](https://greybook.cern.ch/experiment/detail?id=ALICE) experiment at [CERN](https://greybook.cern.ch/institute/detail?id=076486) while serving as a Lecturer at [COMSATS University Islamabad](https://islamabad.comsats.edu.pk/) (advised by [Prof. Arshad Saleem Bhatti](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=nb-LHYEAAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate)), and a joint Master’s thesis between [Tohoku University](https://www.is.tohoku.ac.jp/en/) and [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/en) (advised by [Prof. Satoshi Tadokoro](https://scholar.google.co.jp/citations?user=909K-EUAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Prof. Daniele Nardi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=xZwripcAAAAJ&hl=en)).
